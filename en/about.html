About Rocrown

In the roofing business since 2008, Rocrown is a family-run manufacturer based in Qingyuan, China. Across three production bases and 12 production lines, we make roof tiles and sandwich panels built for tropical climates.

Our story

Our family has worked in roofing since 2008. The factory — Qingyuan Rocrown Building Materials Co., Ltd. — was formally established in 2012, and the second generation of the family runs the business today. For years we supplied the Chinese domestic market; we now bring the same product lines to importers and distributors overseas.

Why Rocrown — the difference is in the materials

Every Rocrown roof is built for the tropics — intense sun, heavy rain, year-round humidity and coastal salt air. The difference is in the materials: color steel, aluminum and stainless steel coil from brand-name mills for our tiles, so they last longer with virtually no color variation; virgin material, never recycled scrap, for our resin and PVC tiles; and quality brand-name adhesives in our sandwich panels, for consistent quality and a more environmentally sound product.

What we make

  • Roof tiles — ASA synthetic resin tiles, PVC and UPVC tiles, colour steel, aluminium and stainless steel tiles, stone coated metal tiles
  • Sandwich panels — rock wool, PU, EPS and aluminium sandwich panels for industrial and commercial buildings
  • Transparent roofing — FRP and polycarbonate sheets
  • PVC gutters, which also serve as planting troughs for farms and greenhouses

OEM and ODM orders are both welcome. Quality inspection reports are available for most of our main products — just ask when you send your inquiry.

Work with the factory, not a middleman

We supply distributors, dealers and long-term trade partners across Southeast Asia, Africa, the Middle East and South America — and larger-volume orders are always welcome.
