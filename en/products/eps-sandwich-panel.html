Frequently asked questions

How do items 10 and 11 differ?

Item 10 is listed as a roof panel with 950 or 1150 mm effective width. Item 11 has 950 or 1050 mm effective width and flat or ribbed surfaces.

What information should I include in an inquiry?

Send item, effective width, core and facing thicknesses, surface and lengths, plus the order quantity and destination port.
