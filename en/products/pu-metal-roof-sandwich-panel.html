Frequently asked questions

How should I specify panel thickness on a quote?

Provide the desired overall panel thickness and the steel facing thickness as separate values, together with the cut lengths.

What information should I include in an inquiry?

Send overall thickness, steel facing thickness, finish, roof drawings and lengths, plus the order quantity and destination port.
