ASA Synthetic Resin Roof Tile

Rocrown's ASA synthetic resin roof tiles are made from quality raw materials, with an ASA surface layer that keeps colours rich under strong sun. Choose the premium grade in 100% virgin material, or an economical grade for budget projects. Each tile is 1050 mm wide with 960 mm effective coverage — 2.5 mm and 3.0 mm as standard, any thickness from 2.0 to 3.0 mm made to order — cut to length in 219 mm steps for homes, villas, warehouses and farm buildings across tropical climates.

Why buyers choose ASA synthetic resin roof tiles

ASA surface layer The ASA surface is what stands between the tile and the sun. It holds the colour under strong UV, so roofs do not fade patchy after a few hot seasons.
Material grades Made from quality raw materials, with a premium grade in 100% virgin material and an economical grade available.
Light and fast to install Light for its coverage, easy to cut and fix, and no painting once it is up. A small crew can finish a roof quickly, which keeps your project labour costs down.
Factory-direct supply Supplied by the manufacturer, not a trading middleman — stable quality and lead times of 5–15 days for regular orders.

Frequently asked questions

What is an ASA synthetic resin roof tile made from?

The tile has an ASA surface layer over a synthetic resin body. Rocrown makes it from quality raw materials, with a premium grade in 100% virgin material and an economical grade available.

What colours do ASA resin roof tiles come in?

Six regular colours: jujube red, brick red, blue, grey, blue-grey and green. Confirm the colour against a sample before ordering.

How long does production take?

Lead times are 5–15 days for regular orders.

Who does Rocrown supply?

We supply distributors, dealers and long-term trade partners across Southeast Asia, Africa, the Middle East and South America — and larger-volume orders are always welcome.

What do I need to send for a quotation?

Send your roof size or total area (m²) or planned quantity, your colour choice, the destination country and nearest port, and the project type. We confirm the exact specification before production.

How much do ASA synthetic resin roof tiles cost?

Price depends on thickness (2.5/3.0 mm standard, or any thickness from 2.0 to 3.0 mm made to order), colour, grade and quantity. Send us your roof size or quantity and destination port, and we will quote for you.

What is the difference between ASA resin roof tiles and regular resin tiles?

The ASA surface layer is what protects the colour. Regular resin tiles can fade quickly under strong sun; the ASA layer helps the colour last, which is why ASA tiles are the standard choice for tropical climates.
