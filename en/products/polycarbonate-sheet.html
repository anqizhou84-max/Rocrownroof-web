Frequently asked questions

Does the solid PC sheet use the same thickness options as hollow sheet?

No. Item 29 is listed at 1-15 mm. Item 30 has separate thickness options for twinwall and for fourwall or honeycomb construction.

What length is listed for hollow polycarbonate sheet?

Item 30 is listed in 6 m or custom lengths. Item 29 is listed with custom lengths.
