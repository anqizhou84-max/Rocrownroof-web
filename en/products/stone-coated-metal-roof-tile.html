Frequently asked questions

How do item 31 and the integrated tiles differ in length?

Item 31 is listed at 420 mm long. Integrated items 32 to 34 are listed in lengths of 1000-6000 mm.

Can I use the listed width as effective coverage?

The catalogue lists widths, not effective coverage. Confirm the side lap and net coverage for the selected profile before estimating quantities.
