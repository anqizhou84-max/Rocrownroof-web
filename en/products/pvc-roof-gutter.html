Frequently asked questions

Can these channels be used for roof drainage and planting?

Yes. Use the gutter profiles to collect and direct roof runoff, or select one of the listed planting channel profiles for growing. Choose the profile for the intended application.

How do the two 140 × 160 gutter options differ?

Both share the listed dimensions and 2.5 mm thickness. The regular PVC option weighs 13.1 kg per piece. The ASA material option weighs 13 kg per piece.

What do the two heights on the small unequal-side gutter mean?

The 165 mm and 95 mm values are the heights of its two sides, not a range.

What does the listed weight refer to?

The weight is in kilograms per piece. The specification sheet does not state a piece length, so confirm the supplied length with your quote.
