Frequently asked questions

What is the listed width difference between PIR and PUR?

Item 20 PIR lists 1120 mm effective width; item 21 PUR lists 960 mm. Confirm the exact joint and panel layout for your project.

What information should I include in an inquiry?

Send pIR or PUR, panel dimensions, overall and facing thicknesses, room drawings and operating temperature, plus the order quantity and destination port.
