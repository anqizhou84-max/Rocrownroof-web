Frequently asked questions

Do 16, 18, 20 and 25 mm refer to the steel facing?

No. These are overall panel thicknesses. The steel facing thickness is a separate specification; ask for it when requesting a quote.

What width should I use for a preliminary takeoff?

Use the listed effective width of 383 mm and confirm the required cuts, corners and openings against your drawings.
