Aluminum Roofing Sheet

Lightweight, corrosion-resistant aluminum sheets are available in the same YX profiles as our prepainted steel sheets. Choose from a range of standard colors and request sheets cut to your required length.

View specifications

Product highlights

  • Aluminum coils are sourced from brand-name mills for consistent color, with minimal visible variation.
  • Lightweight sheets are easier to handle on site.
  • Aluminum is corrosion resistant and does not form iron rust.

Frequently asked questions

What thicknesses are available?

Aluminum roofing sheets are available in 0.5-0.8 mm thickness.

Can you cut the sheets to my required length?

Yes. Send the required sheet lengths with your selected YX profile when requesting a quote.

Which colors can I order?

A range of standard colors is available. Confirm the color and finish for your order.

Is the drawing width the same as installed coverage?

Not necessarily. The table shows the width on the profile drawing. Confirm the effective installed coverage before calculating quantities.
